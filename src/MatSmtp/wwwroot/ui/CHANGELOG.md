# Changelog of MatUi

The toolkit that the Mat apps copy: the CSS (`matui.css`), the script (`matui.js`, `matui.boot.js`) and the icon sprite (`matui-icons.svg`).
This file says what changed in which version, so that an app that carries a copy knows what it takes with an update and what it has to do.
How the numbers work, and how an app updates: [docs/VERSIONING.md](docs/VERSIONING.md).

The format is [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) with two sections of our own:

- **Breaking**: what an app has to change in its own code or markup when it takes the version (a class that was renamed or removed, a changed `data-` attribute, a changed behaviour that apps rely on).
- **Migration**: the steps for it. A version with a Breaking section needs a Migration section.

A change that an app can see is written down under **Unreleased** in the pull request that makes it. `node tools/bump.js <patch|minor|major>` turns Unreleased into the next version.

## [Unreleased]
### Added

- The colour of a new app, MatIdentity (the single sign-on of the family): `[data-app="matidentity"]` sets `--mat-app-accent: #a21a50` (white text on it passes AA). Nothing to do for the other apps but copy the files.
- The colour of a new app, MatSMTP (the mail relay of the family): `[data-app="matsmtp"]` sets `--mat-app-accent: #f5b800` (sun yellow), `--mat-app-on-accent: #0b1020` (dark text on it, 10.6:1) and `--mat-app-accent-text: #8a6100` (links, 5.5:1 on white). Nothing to do for the other apps but copy the files.

### Changed

- The empty state of a list has the frame of the table whose place it takes (`LIST-11`): `.mat-list > .mat-empty` gets the surface, the line, the radius (`--mat-table-radius`) and the shadow of `.mat-table-wrap`, so a list without rows keeps its shape. Outside a list the new modifier `mat-empty--framed` gives an empty state the same frame; in a card it stays without one. Nothing to do for an app but copy the files; an app that drew its own frame around an empty state in a list can drop it.
- A list with nothing yet: `mat-empty--first` on its empty state (the one with the primary action, not the one of an empty search) makes the toolkit hide the same primary button in the actions row below it (`LIST-11`); the other actions stay, a row with nothing else goes. Opt-in: without the modifier nothing changes. An app that left out its actions row by hand for an empty list can render it always and add the modifier.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-15
### Added

- A new icon in the sprite, `mail-m` (an envelope whose flap is an M): the glyph of the MatMail logo (`IDENT-03`, one glyph per app logo). `<svg class="mat-icon"><use href="#mat-i-mail-m"></use></svg>`. Nothing to do for an app but copy the files; the envelope `mail` stays.
- A new icon in the sprite, `paperclip` (a paper clip): the glyph of the MatPaper logo (`IDENT-03`, one glyph per app logo, like `whale` for MatDock). `<svg class="mat-icon"><use href="#mat-i-paperclip"></use></svg>`. Nothing to do for an app but copy the files; the registry of this site uses it for MatPaper instead of `file-text`.
- The icon action of a group heading (`mat-nav__group-action`) is marked like a current item when it is a link to the current page (`SHELL-02`, `COLOR-08`): put `aria-current="page"` (or `is-active`) on it and it gets the tint of the accent behind it and the accent on its icon, as the current item of the menu does. Without it nothing changes. `NavSet.Contains` (and so `ShellModel.CurrentMenu`) now also counts the address of a group action as a page of its menu, because that page is usually no item; the sample marks it with `?groupaction=1&area=lists`.
- A new icon in the sprite, `progress-play` (a progress ring with a play triangle): the glyph of the MatTrakr logo (`IDENT-03`, one glyph per app logo, like `whale` for MatDock). `<svg class="mat-icon"><use href="#mat-i-progress-play"></use></svg>`. Nothing to do for an app but copy the files; the registry of this site uses it for MatTrakr instead of `tv`.
- A group heading in the menu can carry one small icon action at its right end (`SHELL-02`): a gear to manage the list below it, a plus to add to it, instead of a Manage or New item below the list. Markup: `<li class="mat-nav__group mat-nav__group--action" role="presentation"><span class="mat-nav__group-title">Lists</span><a class="mat-nav__group-action" href="…" aria-label="Manage lists">icon</a></li>`, or a `<button class="mat-nav__group-action" data-menu-toggle="id" aria-haspopup="menu">` that opens a menu. The row keeps its height (28 px target, 44 px on a touch screen). `matui.js` needs no change. `NavGroup.Action` / `NavGroupAction` in the shell model of this site; the preview shows it with `?groupaction=1`.
- Choices in the popup of the menu switcher (`SHELL-15`): a context that changes what the whole app shows, such as the tenant, is a closed parent (`mat-menu__parent`, `data-nav-group`) with a form button for each choice below it (`<form class="mat-menu__form" method="post">` around `<button class="mat-menu__item mat-menu__item--sub" role="menuitemradio" aria-checked>`; the new `mat-menu__form` takes no room of its own). The popup lists the menus first, then the choices, then the menus that are last (the administration; `NavSet.Last`). Markup only, `matui.js` needs no change. `ShellModel.Choices` / `SwitcherChoices` in the shell model of this site.
- A menu in the switcher can have a start page (`SHELL-15`): an entry that is a link (`<a class="mat-menu__item" href="…" role="menuitemradio" data-nav-set="id" data-nav-title="…">`) loads its page instead of swapping the items, and the page that loads shows its own menu. Without `href` an entry is a button and swaps the items as before; `matui.js` leaves a link alone (a click with a modifier key opens a new tab and changes nothing here), and the Space key chooses a link in an open menu as it does a button. For an app whose menus are areas with a page each (mail, administration, account), where swapping the items would leave the content of the old area on the screen. `NavSet.Href` in the shell model of this site; a menu with a start page needs no hidden panel in the pages of the other menus.
- The toolkit has a version of its own (`MatUi.version`, the first line of `matui.css` and the header of `matui.js`, `/ui/manifest.json`, `/api/version`), this changelog (also at `/ui/CHANGELOG.md`, `/api/changelog` and on the Changelog page) and a guide for updating an app (docs/VERSIONING.md), with `tools/bump.js` that makes a version and `tools/matui-check.js` that compares an app's copy with the toolkit. The archive an app copies holds the changelog and `matui.manifest.json` with the SHA-256 of every file.
- Menu switcher (`SHELL-15`): it can sit under the logo, at the foot of the menu directly above the user, at both places (`mat-nav__switch--foot`, `data-menu-place="top"`) or at the end of the menu, after the panels (`mat-nav__switch--end`), and menus can be grouped below a parent in its popup (`data-nav-group`, `data-nav-parent`, `mat-menu__parent`, `mat-menu__sub`).
- A main action in the menu: `mat-nav__item--primary`, the first item, filled with the accent, one per app (`SHELL-19`); the main action of the header bar is one icon in the accent colour, `mat-btn--primary mat-btn--icon` last in `mat-topbar__actions`, and a second action beside it is `mat-btn--secondary mat-btn--icon` (`SHELL-17`); the gap and the missing negative margin of the bar apply to both (`:has(> .mat-btn--primary, > .mat-btn--secondary)`).
- Colour schemes (`COLOR-09`): `data-scheme="paper|contrast|oled"` on `<html>` next to `data-theme`, an opt-in change of the surfaces only (the accent never changes); `MatUi.scheme.get/set`, `data-scheme-set`, `data-scheme-save-url`, the event `mat:scheme`, and `matui.boot.js` applies the saved scheme before the first paint. The standard look is unchanged.
- A select can create what is missing and suggest (`FORM-16`): `data-create-url` (a first row Create "text") and `data-suggest-url` (Suggestions above All), both opt-in, with `MatUi.picker.set`, `MatUi.picker.addOption` and `MatUi.picker.suggest`.
- Colours a user chose (`COLOR-10`): `data-colour="#rrggbb"` on `mat-tag` (a dot before the text), the new `mat-dot`, `mat-icon` and an option of a select picker; `MatUi.refresh(scope)` for markup added later.
- Tag input: `data-picker="tags"`, a field of chips with suggestions (`FORM-15`).
- Dropdown with a list of checkboxes: `data-picker="select" data-multiple` with `data-select-all` (`FORM-15`).
- Appointment picker: `data-picker="slot"` shows the days that have a free time and then the times of the chosen day; what is taken is marked or hidden (`data-unavailable`), `data-min`, `data-max` and `data-min-notice` keep out the past, Next free appointment, and availability that is loaded per month (`mat:slots-needed`, `MatUi.picker.setSlots`). It posts `2026-10-14T09:30` (`FORM-18`).
- Calendar: `mat-calendar` with `data-calendar`, a calendar that is always shown, for one day, a period or several days, with minimum and maximum, disabled dates and weekdays, events with an agenda and week numbers (`FORM-18`).
- The date pickers have a jump to a month and a year, and `data-min` and `data-max` also take `today`, `tomorrow`, `+2d`, `+3m` and the like.
- Schedule: `mat-schedule` with `data-schedule`, the events one below another in the views Agenda, Day, Week, Month and Year, with calendar weeks, paging by buttons, keys and swipe, and events that are loaded for the visible period (`LIST-14`).
- Lists of rows: `mat-itemlist` and `mat-item` for contacts, messages, settings, files, tasks and the like, with sliding actions, reordering, a filter and select all (`LIST-15`).
- Carousel: `mat-carousel` with `data-carousel`, `data-loop` and `data-autoplay` (`LIST-13`).
- Groups in a table: `tbody.mat-group` with a heading row that opens and closes the group (`data-groups`, `data-group`, `data-group-toggle`, `data-group-fixed`, `data-groups-set`), the rows hanging on a line and the add row (`mat-group__add`) as the last row of the group; the choice is remembered (`LIST-16`).
- Number stepper: `mat-stepper` with `data-stepper` (`FORM-20`).
- Slider and range slider with two thumbs: `mat-slider` with `data-slider` (`FORM-17`).
- Code input: `mat-otp` with `data-otp`, for one-time codes and PINs (`FORM-19`).
- Documented component properties (`SPACE-06`): 68 custom properties `--mat-<component>-<aspect>` that change the shape of a control without overriding its classes: `--mat-btn-radius`, `--mat-btn-height`, `--mat-btn-pad-x`, `--mat-btn-border-width`, `--mat-btn-font-weight`, `--mat-input-*` (radius, height, pad-x, border-width), `--mat-card-*`, `--mat-dialog-*`, `--mat-menu-*`, `--mat-tag-*`, `--mat-pill-*`, `--mat-badge-*`, `--mat-tabs-*`, `--mat-table-*`, `--mat-alert-*`, `--mat-toast-*`, `--mat-switch-*` (width, height, thumb), `--mat-check-*`, `--mat-nav-item-*` and `--mat-topbar-*`. Set one on `:root` (every app), on `[data-app]` (one app) or on one element. None has a value in the toolkit: every use is `var(--mat-btn-radius, var(--mat-radius))`, so an app that sets none sees no change (a check in a browser compares the computed styles of every example). A height is a floor: keep it at 44 px or more on a touch screen. The list with types and defaults is in the CSS (one `@prop` line above the rule), on the Foundations > Tokens page, on the page of each component and at `/api/properties`.
- A brand in `matui.css` (`COLOR-11`): the tokens a company sets in the MatDesign Studio (colours, radii, spacing, sizes, fonts, line heights, motion) are written after everything else in the apps part, light on `:root` and the dark set twice and identical (`:root[data-theme="dark"]` and `prefers-color-scheme: dark`), within limits (touch targets of 44 px, a body text of 14 px or more, a line height of 1.2 to 2, radii of 0 to 24 px) and checked for AA before they are saved or released. A brand that sets nothing writes nothing, so `matui.css` is byte-identical to before: nothing to do for an app but copy the files.
- Rating: `mat-rating` with `data-rating`, stars (or `mat-rating--heart`, `mat-rating--dot`) to give a value, a group of native radio buttons in a fieldset that the script draws as shapes, with a preview under the pointer, half steps (twice the radios), `data-clearable`, `data-show-value`, the sizes `--sm` and `--lg` and the event `mat:rating-change`; and, with `mat-rating--readonly` and `data-value`, an average that is shown, partly filled, with `data-count` and a label in words (`FORM-22`). `MatUi.rating.get/set/init`, texts `data-rt-<name>` on `<html>`. Two new icons in the sprite, `heart` and `circle`. Nothing to do for an app that does not use it but copy the files.
- Tree view: `ul.mat-tree[data-tree]` of `li.mat-tree__item` (a row `mat-tree__row` with `mat-tree__icon`, `mat-tree__label`, `mat-tree__count`, `mat-tree__actions`, and a nested `ul.mat-tree__group`) for folders, categories and any hierarchy (`LIST-17`). It follows the ARIA tree pattern (roles, `aria-level`, `aria-setsize`, `aria-posinset`, `aria-expanded`, one Tab stop, the arrow keys, Home, End, `*`, Enter, Space and typing a name), has one chosen item (`aria-selected`) or, with `data-multiple`, boxes with a parent that is in between (`aria-checked="mixed"`), a filter (`data-tree-filter`), open and close all (`data-tree-set`), remembered state (`data-tree-key`), children loaded on demand (`data-lazy`, `mat:tree-load`, `MatUi.tree.setChildren`) and the events `mat:tree-select`, `mat:tree-toggle`, `mat:tree-filter`; script `MatUi.tree`, texts `TR_TEXT` in English and German (`data-tr-<name>`). Modifiers `mat-tree--compact` and `mat-tree--no-lines`.
- A new icon in the sprite, `folder-open`: the open state of a folder in the tree view (`<svg class="mat-icon"><use href="#mat-i-folder-open"></use></svg>`).
- File upload: `mat-upload` with `data-upload`, a drop zone around a real file input, with the list of the chosen files, limits and refusals in words (`FORM-21`). Markup: `<div class="mat-upload" data-upload data-max-files="5" data-max-size="10MB"><input class="mat-upload__input" type="file" id="docs" name="docs" multiple accept=".pdf,.png"></div>`; without the script it is the native file field (`required`, `accept`, `multiple` work either way). The script checks `accept`, `data-max-size` and `data-max-files`, names every refused file with the reason, keeps the real input equal to the list (so the form posts what the list shows), shows thumbnails of images (`data-preview="none"` turns them off) and adds pasted images with `data-paste`. Events `mat:upload-change` and `mat:upload-refused`; for an app that uploads by itself `MatUi.upload.setProgress(el, file, 0..100)`, `.setError`, `.files`, `.add`, `.remove`, `.clear`. Texts in English and German (`data-up-<name>` on `<html>` changes one). Nothing to do for an app but copy the files.
- Overrides in `matui.css` (`SPACE-06`, `COLOR-11`), written after the brand so that the order is the toolkit's defaults, the brand, the app and, last, a colour scheme the user chose: the component properties of the brand on `:root` (a height of a control that is pressed, under 44 px, only inside `@media (pointer: fine)`, so a touch screen keeps the 44 px of `--mat-control-h`, `MOBILE-04`), the tokens the brand changes inside a colour scheme (`:root[data-scheme="paper"] { ... }` with the dark set twice and identical, black has dark values only) and what a single app sets for itself on `[data-app="id"]` (shape, spacing, type and motion tokens, and component properties, the dark set twice and identical). Nothing set writes nothing, so `matui.css` is byte-identical to before: nothing to do for an app but copy the files.
- White label build (`CODE-09`, docs/PRODUCT.md stage 15): a release of the Studio can carry the toolkit under a company's own class prefix and product name (`acme-btn`, `--acme-radius`, `data-acme-sprite`, `acme:theme`, the keys `acme-theme`, the sprite ids `acme-i-check`, `window.AcmeUi`, the files `acmeui.css`, `acmeui.js`, `acmeui.boot.js`, `acmeui-icons.svg`, `acmeui.manifest.json`). The rename is made by the build on the copy, on token boundaries, and the copy is checked before it is published; the source of the toolkit and every release without a setting are unchanged, so there is nothing to do for an app that uses `mat-` and `MatUi`. An app that copies a white-labelled archive uses the names of that archive. `tools/matui-check.js` reads the product and the file names from the manifest, so it checks such a copy too.

- The files of a brand in `matui.css` and in the archive (`IDENT-08`): the font faces a company uploads in the MatDesign Studio are written as `@font-face` rules at the end of `matui.css` (`font-display: swap`, `src: url("brand/fonts/<name>") format("woff2")`, **relative to the stylesheet**; `css/matui.apps.css` has `../brand/fonts/<name>`), and the archive and every release carry the files (logo, word mark, favicon, app icon, fonts) in `brand/` with their SHA-256 in the manifest. An app that wants them copies `brand/` next to `matui.css` (docs/ADOPTING.md, 6b); one that does not, changes nothing: a brand without assets writes nothing and every file is what it was. A family is used by naming it in a token of the brand (`--mat-font: "Acme Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`).
- `mat-brandmark`: the image of a brand's logo or word mark (`<img class="mat-brandmark" src alt width height>`, 38 px tall; `mat-brandmark--word` 22 px). A variant for dark surfaces is a second image, `mat-brandmark mat-brandmark--light` and `mat-brandmark mat-brandmark--dark`: the CSS shows one of them by `data-theme` and, without it, by `prefers-color-scheme` (the dark rules are written twice, like the tokens), no script and no inline style. `mat-nav__brand-link--logo-only` on the link of the menu header keeps the name and the tagline for screen readers only, for a wide logo that carries the name itself. Nothing to do for an app that has no brand image.

### Changed

- The accent of MatMail is graphite, `#475569` (it was `#1a56db`): the colour MatMail started with, and its new logo (the M envelope, glyph `mail-m`) follows it (`IDENT-03`): `[data-app="matmail"]` in `matui.apps.css` and the registry of this site. MatMail takes it with its next copy of the toolkit and changes its `theme-color` and `Ui.App.Accent` with it.
- The accent of Matddns is a dark blue, `#204db6` (it was `#4173d1`), to go with its new icon: `[data-app="matddns"]` in `matui.apps.css` and the registry of this site. It is the darkest blue that still holds AA in both themes (white text on it in light, the toolkit's ink on its lightened form in dark). Matddns takes it with its next copy of the toolkit and changes its `theme-color` and `Ui.App.Accent` with it.
- `MatUi.version` is the version of the toolkit (a string such as `0.1.0`); it used to be the fixed string `1`.
- The first line of `matui.css` is `/* MatUi <version> (<hash>) - build <build> - ... */`; the build that it used to name first is now second.
- The panels of the pickers only fade in: they no longer start lower and smaller and grow into place (`mat-picker-in` replaces `mat-menu-in` for them).
- The date pickers and the calendar draw their months with the same code; nothing that an app writes has changed.
- The label of the theme button, AM and PM of a 12-hour time and the hint of a typed time are texts of `PK_TEXT` in English and German, chosen by `<html lang>`, and can be changed with `data-theme-label`, `data-theme-name-system|light|dark` and `data-pk-<name>` on `<html>`; they were English only.
- `data-selectable` also marks cards and tiles: the item of a ticked check is `is-selected` (its table row, its `mat-tile`, or the nearest element with `data-select-item`), a tint behind the whole item.
- `--mat-accent-text` is mixed with `--mat-text` by `--mat-accent-text-keep` (100 % in the standard look, so nothing changes there; a colour scheme sets that one share).
- A toast is shown above an open dialog, inside the topmost one, instead of behind it.
- The site that serves the toolkit names its own surfaces after the company that runs it (`IDENT-09`, stage 14 of docs/PRODUCT.md): the README of the archive and of a release says "Made by <site name>" and "the UI toolkit of the <company> apps", and the apps part of `matui.css` of a company that started from the blank template has one rule more, `[data-app="this-site"] { --mat-app-accent: ...; }`, for the colour of the site itself. An instance that has no company, and every release made before, is the demo and is unchanged: no file of MatUi differs, nothing to do for an app.

### Fixed

- The index of a long page ("On this page") can be scrolled by the user again: it followed the page on every scroll event and pulled itself back.
- The `code` inside a `.mat-code` block has no padding of its own: the padding of an inline code moved the first line of a block to the right.
- A submit button with `formnovalidate` sends the form without the check of the toolkit, as in the browser (`FORM-05`), also after a `data-confirm` on the form, which now submits again with the button that was pressed.
- The hit area of what was smaller than 44 px on a touch screen is 44 px (`MOBILE-04`: the part of the screen that takes the tap, not the drawn mark; the look does not change and nothing changes with a mouse, every rule is written for `(pointer: coarse)`). The label of a check, a radio button and a switch (it was 36 px; the mark stays where it was and keeps following `--mat-check-size` and `--mat-switch-*`, the target does not depend on them), the check of a card head, the X of a chip, a tag and an alert or a toast, the clear button of a picker, the sub items of the menu, the links of the breadcrumb and of the footer, the buttons of the pager, the segmented control, the pills, the dots of a carousel, the sort links of a table, the index links of a long page, the options and days and times and swatches and the hue slider in the panels of the pickers (the swatch is drawn in the middle of its cell). The cell around the check of a table row and a row of a list that is a field forward a tap to the check or the field (`matui.js`). A rating of seven or more shapes that has no room for 44 px shapes on a phone is set in two rows (`is-wrapped`, `--mat-rating-cols`, set by the script); the filled part and the pointer follow. Nothing to do for an app but copy the files; an app's own small buttons need the same: a padding or a centred `::before` of 44 px around the mark (docs/ADOPTING.md, "Touch targets").

## [0.1.0]

The toolkit as the apps copy it before it had a version: the shell with the menu (badges, submenus, switcher), the header bar and the footer; buttons, forms and the pickers (select with pictures, date and period, time and time range, colour); lists with toolbar, table and pager; cards, tabs, dialogs, alerts, toasts and the other content components; the layout system and the utilities; the icon sprite and the optional icon library; the tokens for light and dark and the accent of every app.
